An optional stable name for your supernode, so saved connections and new invites still find it after its IP address changes. $10 a year for up to 10 names, which helps fund development. Sign-ups are in test mode; no payments are taken yet. Your own DNS or a JSON file on your own website works too.

A few common questions

Good to know.

Can a supernode read my conversations?

No. It can see who is connected and when, and the names of rooms and of files shared in them, but not what is said or sent.

Do I have to run a supernode?

No. You can use someone else’s. One-to-one conversations can connect directly when your network allows; rooms need a supernode.

Does it work when someone is offline?

No. Messages are delivered only while both sides are online; nothing is stored for later delivery.

Was AI used in development?

Yes. Tools including Claude, Cursor, Codex, and Grok have been used during development.
